Privacy Policy

App: Styne Quality (package: com.guntonrolf.qualityreporter)
Developer: Gunton Rolf, independent app developer
Effective date: 11 November 2026

This policy explains how the Styne Quality mobile application handles information entered or used while preparing and submitting non-conformance reports (NCRs).

1. Information handled by the app

Depending on the report, the app may access or process:

  • Names, job or project details, site and organisation information, report descriptions and other information entered by users.
  • Photographs selected or captured for NCR evidence, which may contain people, identifying information or location details.
  • Handwritten signatures and associated report information.
  • Text entered using the device's keyboard or speech-to-text features.
  • PDF documents generated from report information.

Users should only enter or attach information needed for legitimate NCR reporting purposes.

2. How information is used

The app uses information supplied by a user to prepare an NCR, attach supporting evidence, obtain signatures where applicable, and create a PDF report for submission. It is not intended to use NCR content for advertising or sale.

3. Local storage and developer access

The app is designed to operate offline and prepare reports on the Android device. It does not automatically upload NCR reports to a server operated by Gunton Rolf. Information may remain on the device while the app retains draft data or generated files. Access to such information depends on device access controls and where files are saved or shared.

Gunton Rolf does not receive submitted NCR contents merely because the app is installed or used. If an email is addressed to the developer, the developer will receive the information included in that message.

4. Emailing PDFs and third-party services

When a user chooses to submit or share an NCR, the app provides the generated PDF to the email application or sharing service selected on the device. Sending the email may transfer the PDF, email addresses and message contents to the chosen email provider and the intended recipients. Those parties handle information under their own privacy policies and applicable organisational rules.

If speech-to-text is used, the Android device's chosen voice recognition service may process spoken input, potentially using an external service, according to the device's settings and the service provider's terms. The app does not itself operate a voice transcription server.

5. Device permissions

Depending on the features used and Android version, the app may request access to the camera or photographs for supporting images, the microphone for dictation, and file access needed to create or share PDFs. These features are used for preparation and submission of NCRs.

6. Security

Information held on the device is subject to the security protections of the Android device and the app's storage arrangements. Users and organisations should protect devices with suitable screen locks, control access to reports, and use appropriately secured email accounts and recipients. Normal email is not necessarily end-to-end encrypted.

7. Retention and deletion

Locally saved drafts, reports or exported PDFs can remain on the device until removed through the relevant app or device file-management functions. Uninstalling the app may remove app-private data but may not remove copies exported elsewhere, stored in backups or attached to sent emails.

After a report is emailed, copies may remain in the sender's email account, the recipient's account and the recipient organisation's record systems according to their retention policies. Gunton Rolf cannot delete those copies remotely. Requests about reports already sent to an employer, customer or other organisation should be directed to that organisation.

8. Children

Styne Quality is intended for workplace reporting and is not designed for or directed towards children. Its use on a supervised Android device does not change the app's intended audience.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality or information-handling practices change. The latest version will be published at this webpage with an updated effective date.
